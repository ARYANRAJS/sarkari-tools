Frequently Asked Questions: UPI & Custom QR Codes

1

Why Error Correction Level H Matters for Logos

Level H recovers up to 30% of covered or obscured QR data. This guarantees that placing a PhonePe, GPay, or custom shop logo in the center will never cause scanning failures.

2

What is vCard 3.0 Standard?

vCard 3.0 allows anyone scanning your visiting card with an iPhone or Android camera to instantly save your phone number, email, company, and location directly into their contacts.

3

100% Client-Side Privacy

Your UPI ID, bank payee names, Wi-Fi passwords, and scanned camera frames never leave your device. All rendering is performed using the HTML5 Canvas API in your browser.
