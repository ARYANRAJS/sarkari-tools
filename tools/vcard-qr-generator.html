Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What information can I store inside a vCard QR code?

You can store your full name, mobile number, office landline, email address, company or business name, designation, website URL, and office or shop address.

Q: Will this QR code expire?

Never! This is a 100% static vCard QR code. The contact information is encoded directly into the pixel pattern. It will work forever without any recurring monthly subscriptions.

Q: What is the recommended size for printing on visiting cards?

For standard business cards (3.5 × 2 inches), print the QR code at a minimum size of 0.8 × 0.8 inches (2 × 2 cm) to ensure clean readability by all budget smartphone cameras.
