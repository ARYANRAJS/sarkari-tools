Why are Robots.txt & XML Sitemaps Essential?

The robots.txt file is the first file search engines read when visiting your site. It controls where Googlebot is allowed to go, preventing it from wasting crawl budget on private admin panels, backend scripts, or duplicate search parameter queries. An XML Sitemap acts as a comprehensive map of your website, informing Google of newly published tools, articles, and pages so they get indexed within hours instead of weeks.

Where to Place These Files:

  • Upload robots.txt directly to your root directory (e.g. https://yourdomain.com/robots.txt).
  • Upload sitemap.xml directly to your root directory (e.g. https://yourdomain.com/sitemap.xml).
  • Submit your sitemap URL directly in Google Search Console under the Sitemaps tab.
