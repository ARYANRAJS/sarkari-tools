Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do I get my exact shop or venue link from Google Maps?

Open Google Maps on your phone or computer, search for your location, click 'Share' > 'Copy link' (it looks like https://maps.app.goo.gl/xxxx), and paste it into the input box above.

Q: Can I use latitude and longitude coordinates?

Yes! You can enter coordinates like 28.6139, 77.2090, and the scanner will route directly to that exact GPS point.
