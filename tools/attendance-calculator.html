University Attendance Rules & Calculations

1

Why 75% Attendance is Mandatory

University Grants Commission (UGC) and state colleges mandate minimum 75% attendance to receive your examination admit card.

2

Consecutive Class Attendance Formula

If attendance < 75%, the formula calculates: `Classes to attend = (0.75 × Total - Attended) / 0.25`.

3

Bunk / Safe Leave Calculation

If attendance > 75%, it calculates how many classes you can skip before your percentage drops below 75%.
