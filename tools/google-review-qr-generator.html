Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What if I don't have a custom Google shortlink yet?

You can paste your normal Google Maps place URL (e.g. https://maps.app.goo.gl/...). It will open your business page on Google Maps where users can tap 'Write a Review'.

Q: Can I print this on cash receipts or delivery bags?

Yes! Download the QR code as PNG and insert it into your thermal bill printer template or shopping bag stamps with the text "Scan to review us on Google".
