Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Why does my browser ask for camera permission?

Browsers require user consent to access the webcam for security reasons. Click 'Allow' when the prompt appears. We do not record, store, or stream your camera feed.

Q: What formats of QR codes are supported?

Our scanner supports standard QR codes containing Web URLs, UPI payment URIs (`upi://pay`), vCard contact cards, Wi-Fi credentials (`WIFI:`), plain text, phone numbers, and geo coordinates.

Q: Why won't a blurry or dark QR code scan?

Ensure adequate lighting and hold the code steady so the three large corner square markers (finder patterns) are clearly visible and sharp in the viewfinder.
