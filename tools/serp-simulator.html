Why Does Pixel Width Matter More Than Character Count?

Google displays title tags inside a container of roughly 600 pixels wide on desktop. Because letters like W and M take significantly more pixels than thin letters like I or l, a 55-character title with wide uppercase letters can get cut off, whereas a 65-character title with narrow letters might fit completely! Our simulator renders text onto an off-screen HTML5 Canvas with Google's native Arial 20px font to measure exact pixel width down to a single pixel.
