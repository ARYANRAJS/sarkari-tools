Frequently Asked Questions

A standard A4 sheet can hold up to 30 passport photos (5 columns × 6 rows) with standard 3.5×4.5 cm dimensions. You can also pick standard sequence layouts like 5 photos, 8 photos, or 10 photos if you only need a few prints.
For official government submissions, print on 180 GSM or 210 GSM glossy photo paper at any local photo studio or cyber cafe. The downloaded file is created at 300 DPI for crystal clear studio print quality.
