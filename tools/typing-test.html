Exam Qualification Norms:

  • SSC CHSL English Typing: Minimum 35 WPM (10500 KDPH) with up to 5% allowed mistakes for General, 7% for OBC/SC/ST.
  • SSC CHSL Hindi Typing: Minimum 30 WPM (9000 KDPH).
  • Court Typist / Steno: Minimum 35 - 40 WPM with 95%+ accuracy.

How to Practice for SSC Typing Exams

1

Select Exam Mode

Choose English or Hindi and select 10 or 15 minutes as per official exam guidelines.

2

Type with Rhythm

Look at the screen text and type smoothly. Correct letters turn green, mistakes turn red.

3

Review Qualification

At the end of timer, check whether you cleared the SSC 35 WPM / 30 WPM threshold!

Frequently Asked Questions

Gross WPM is total words typed per minute including mistakes. Net WPM subtracts your errors from the gross words. Government exams score you strictly on Net WPM and overall accuracy percentage.
