Why Color Accessibility (WCAG 2.1) Matters

1

WCAG AA Compliance (4.5:1)

Normal text must have at least a 4.5:1 contrast ratio against the background to be readable for individuals with visual impairments or color blindness.

2

Digital Display vs Print Standards

RGB/HEX powers backlit digital monitors and web pages, while CMYK is required for professional offset printing and brochure publishing.

3

Color Psychology in Branding

Using mathematically balanced complementary and triadic harmonies creates high-converting, professional user interfaces.
