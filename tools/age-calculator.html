Frequently Asked Questions

SSC notifications define a specific reference date (usually 1st January or 1st August of the exam year). Your age must fall within the minimum (usually 18) and maximum (27 or 32) limits on that exact date.
Central government norms prescribe 3 years upper age relaxation for OBC (Non-Creamy Layer), 5 years for SC/ST, and 10 years for Persons with Disabilities (PwD) candidates.
