Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What is the format of the generated WhatsApp link?

The QR code encodes the official Meta WhatsApp Click-to-Chat URI: https://wa.me/<phonenumber>?text=<encoded_message>. This is recognized natively by iOS, Android, macOS, and Windows.

Q: Can I use this for international numbers outside India?

Yes! You can enter any international country code (e.g. 1 for USA, 44 for UK, 971 for UAE) followed by the phone number without leading zeros or plus signs.
